Academic Portfolio

Janith Kavindu Dassanayake

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Southern Illinois University Carbondale. My research focuses on wireless communications, integrated sensing and communication (ISAC), massive MIMO, near-field communication, SWIPT, reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, physical layer security, convex optimization, and learning-based optimization for next-generation wireless networks.

My dissertation research, Waveform and Resource Optimization for Wireless Communications, Sensing, and Energy Transfer, studies waveform, beamformer, and resource allocation methods for secure and efficient communication, sensing, and wireless energy transfer systems.

Selected Research

📡

Optimal Waveform and Beamformer Designs for ISAC

2024 - Present

Developing waveform and beamforming methods for ISAC systems with extremely large antenna arrays, near-field propagation, cell-free architecture, RISs, and repeaters.

🧠

Learning-Based Waveform Designs for ISAC

2024 - Present

Using deep learning to optimize communication-sensing trade-offs, spectral efficiency, target detection accuracy, and interference management in dynamic wireless environments.

⚡

IRS-Aided SWIPT Massive MIMO Systems

2023

Designing intelligent reflective surface assisted SWIPT systems to maximize harvested energy and information rate in massive MIMO communication networks.

🔐

Secure IRS-Aided Massive MIMO

2022 - 2023

Analyzing secrecy rate and detecting active pilot contamination attacks using Neyman-Pearson and generalized likelihood ratio test based detectors.

🌐

Reinforcement Learning for CDN Caching

2023

Developed a multi-agent deep-Q network based cache replacement policy for content delivery networks.

🔋

Microgrid Power Flow Controller

2019 - 2020

Designed and implemented a controller for grid-connected energy storage using MATLAB Simulink and TI C2000 microcontroller hardware.
